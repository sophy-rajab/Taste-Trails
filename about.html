About Taste Trails

A restaurant recommendation and review management system designed for diners in Kenya.

What we solve

Finding a restaurant that matches your cuisine, budget, and location should not take hours of searching friends, social media, or scattered review sites. Taste Trails brings discovery, ratings, and recommendations into one place.

The platform focuses on Kenya’s hospitality scene — helping locals and visitors make informed dining decisions with transparent community feedback.

What you can do

• Create an account and manage your profile

• Search restaurants by cuisine, budget, and location

• Read and leave reviews with star ratings

• Save favourite restaurants

• Get recommendations matched to your preferences

• Open map links for easier navigation
